# Family E (supporting buildings): what can be built from documentary sources and what needs approved visual references

Written 2026-11-09 after the owner's decision: **Family E is on hold until the reference gaps M-02 and M-04 are resolved**; this document only identifies, per asset, what the sources allow, so that the work can start the moment the references arrive. **Nothing in Family E was modelled.** The rows are those of `KANO_TRANSPORT_ASSET_BREAKDOWN.md` section E (district D05 commercial belt, D08 institutional). Families A, B and C are not blocked by it (`TRANSPORT_KIT.md`).

**What the project's own rules say** (handover section 2, rule 6): the owner's generated concept images are the compulsory visual standard but are *private design references*, never textures, never claimed to be verified images of Kano; **no architecture or landmark is invented without a reference**; where none exists, an asset is a clearly flagged *design interpretation* of documented qualitative features. M-02 (modern Kano buildings: shop rows, plaza, apartment block, mid-rise, filling station) and M-04 (commercial streets and markets: shopfronts with shutters and sign bands, kiosks, stalls, bus stops, street views) are the registered gaps (`docs/kano/REFERENCE_REGISTER.md`, gaps table).

## What the owner can decide to unblock the most

1. **Accept generic commercial buildings** (frontage a/b/c and the mid-rise) as *design interpretations of the registered concepts R-022 and R-016*, flagged in the registry and the in-game labels. This does not resolve M-02/M-04: it makes the district buildable with an honest label. **Or** send photographs of Kano commercial frontages and a mid-rise for M-02 and shopfronts for M-04.
2. Send **one set of photographs of a covered market hall** (and a hotel) or say that these two stay out of the game until references exist.
3. Confirm that **generic fictional brands** appear on every canopy, sign band and board (already approved on 2026-11-09 for the transport kit; this extends it to commercial frontages).

## Dependencies on engine and kit work already done

* Sites for Family E sit on the E2 site plates (`ROAD_ENGINE_E2.md`) and, for the commercial belt, on the E5 commercial-district roundabout (`ROAD_ENGINE_E5.md`); text faces, light heads and colliders use the same catalogue fields as the transport kit.
* The palette and 4-material rule of the transport kit (`TRANSPORT_KIT.md` section 2) apply: a frontage is one mesh with at most four materials.
* No asset may be built before its registry entry carries a reference or an explicit `needs_concept` flag (handover section 5, item 5).
