## What the run found and what was changed

* **High beam (real defect).** The earlier design re-aimed and narrowed the two low-beam lamps (420 / 170 m / 38 degrees / 0.3 degrees). In the real build that gave a dimmer road ahead than the low beam. I could not explain it from the lamp numbers alone (the new lamp lines log intensity, range, angle, pitch and height for every lamp, so the next run shows them). Instead of tuning, the design changed to what a real vehicle does: **the low beam stays on and the high beam adds a second pair of lamps** (360 / 170 m / 34 degrees / 12 degrees inner / 0.6 degrees down). Adding light cannot give a darker road. Cost: two more lights, only while the high beam is on (the Low tier allows four per object). `VehicleLights.cs`, `HeadlightSpec`.
* **Indicator checks:** a script bug (fixed).
* **Beacon check:** marginal at 150 m (fixed by distance).
* **All of this is unverified until the next run**: the changes compile only in CI.
