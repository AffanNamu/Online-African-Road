# Kano City: the actual city (owner directive of 2026-11-16)

The city is one route, `ng-kano-city`, generated from the master plan's own road graph (`docs/kano/city/city_plan.json`, KSA-1, 10 km x 10 km) by `tools/kano/make_city.py`. E1, F1 and Phase 1 are test environments, not the city. On the world map: Kano panel, DRIVE HERE, **Kano City** (first).

**Distribution, checked (not just counted).** An audit of where things stand (from the lots the route itself places): fuel stations only on the A2, the arterials and the collectors (none on neighbourhood streets, where the first version had 33 of 93; 50 in all); a neighbourhood mosque within a median 111 m of every home (90 percent within 215 m) and a Friday mosque within a median 470 m (it was 1.2 km with 9 Friday mosques); shops and offices take about half of the ring road's and two thirds of the radials' and the wall road's frontage, homes two thirds of the neighbourhood streets' and all of the estate's; industry only along the A2 and in the transport hub.

**Street lights.** The transport kit's lamp models with their own night light heads: twin-arm lights on the medians of the A2 and the divided arterials, single-arm lights on the collectors, service roads, ramps and round every roundabout, ornamental lanterns on the neighbourhood streets.

**Seen from the driver's seat.** With the buildings instanced, their cull distances are raised (houses 220 to 400 m, villas 260 to 420 m, city buildings 500 to 600 m, Friday mosques 600 to 800 m) and the playable build's prefab LODs now end at the game catalogue's distance instead of the kit's 250 m review distance: before, a street emptied about 100 m ahead on the Low tier. CI drives a neighbourhood street and the flyover at Medium quality and saves the pictures.

Download budget: the route file is written compact and without the frontage fields that equal the engine's defaults (1.0 MB; the Resources budget is 1.5 MB per file and 6 MB in all, of which 5.97 MB are now used). **This is now the limit on growing the city**: aligning the inner band's cross streets into crossroads with the outer band's (tried on 2026-11-16: 90 more street segments, a connected grid) takes the route to 1.09 MB and Resources past 6 MB. The options need a decision: raise the Resources budget for route data, or let the game read a compact route encoding (a loader change). The route's control points alone repeat the same zone, surface, profile and elevation thousands of times. CI run 357 failed on this budget (the first commit of the districts wrote 1.56 MB) and on a catalogue tag count; both fixed in the next commit.

Network: 361 roads, 10 roundabouts, 184 km of streets. Left empty on purpose: the D07 sector (157.5 to 202.5 degrees of the outer band, the traditional suburb: BLOCKED on references in the plan) and everything inside the wall. The route builds in about 0.5 s and places its buildings in about 0.7 s (native). Every city check of the test routes also runs on the city: no building overlaps another or touches a road, corner lots of two roads never meet (each road leaves a 58 m window round every junction), nobody in the crowd stands on a carriageway or inside a building, every pump is off the road, the mix is varied, a golden digest.

## Not built yet (in order of work)
1. **RB-15 batching** (approved 2026-11-16): **seen working** in the build of CI run 358 (bb4bf39): every building model is planned for instancing, none refused, the buildings drawn instanced (on a neighbourhood street 278 registered, the near ones drawn in 13 to 23 draws). Still to measure: frame time on a real phone and desktop GPU.
2. **Traffic through junctions and roundabouts**: traffic now runs on the main road (the A2, over the flyover, to the middle ring roundabout, where a vehicle that reaches the end disappears). Traffic that turns at junctions and goes round the roundabouts is new engine behaviour: **proposed in `docs/TRAFFIC_NETWORK_PROPOSAL.md`, waiting for the owner's approval**.
3. **Roadside environments**: roadside stalls and container shops (family K) now line the shop streets, the middle ring and, busiest, the wall road; still to come: bus shelters with waiting passengers at the kerb (the street rule for bus stops places nothing yet: its offset is inside the default clearance), shrubs and grass on the verges, a mall.
4. The middle ring has no roadside mosque (its stretches between cross streets are shorter than a block and a half); the inner band's cross streets are few where they would meet the outer band's within 1.5 degrees (to be aligned as crossroads).
5. **Pending, by the owner's rule**: the old city inside the wall, Kurmi market, the palace and Central Mosque precinct, Dala Hill. Nothing is built inside the wall and no landmark is invented.
