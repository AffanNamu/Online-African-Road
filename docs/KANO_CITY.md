# Kano City: the actual city (owner directive of 2026-11-16)

The city is one route, `ng-kano-city`, generated from the master plan's own road graph (`docs/kano/city/city_plan.json`, KSA-1, 10 km x 10 km) by `tools/kano/make_city.py`. E1, F1 and Phase 1 are test environments, not the city. On the world map: Kano panel, DRIVE HERE, **Kano City** (first).

**Distribution, checked (not just counted).** An audit of where things stand (from the lots the route itself places): fuel stations only on the A2, the arterials and the collectors (none on neighbourhood streets, where the first version had 33 of 93; 45 in all, a median 355 m apart); a neighbourhood mosque within a median 113 m of every home (90 percent within 215 m), mosques along the ring road (25), the middle ring (40) and the wall road (14) as well, and a Friday mosque within a median 392 m (it was 1.2 km with 9 Friday mosques); shops and offices take about half of the ring road's and two thirds of the radials' and the wall road's frontage, homes two thirds of the neighbourhood streets' and all of the estate's; industry only along the A2 and in the transport hub.

**Street lights.** The transport kit's lamp models with their own night light heads: twin-arm lights on the medians of the A2 and the divided arterials, single-arm lights on the collectors, service roads, ramps and round every roundabout, ornamental lanterns on the neighbourhood streets.

**Seen from the driver's seat.** With the buildings instanced, their cull distances are raised (houses 220 to 400 m, villas 260 to 420 m, city buildings 500 to 600 m, Friday mosques 600 to 800 m) and the playable build's prefab LODs now end at the game catalogue's distance instead of the kit's 250 m review distance: before, a street emptied about 100 m ahead on the Low tier. CI drives a neighbourhood street and the flyover at Medium quality and saves the pictures.

Download budget: the route file is written compact, without the frontage fields that equal the engine's defaults, and **each building mix only once**: the frontage rules name a shared lot list (`mixes`, `mixId`, `anchorsId`; `docs/KANO_CITY_FRONTAGE.md`) instead of repeating it, which took the file from 1.04 MB to 0.6 MB with exactly the same buildings (a test packs the three test routes and finds every lot unchanged). The Resources budget is 1.5 MB per file and 6 MB in all, of which 5.61 MB are now used (the city route 0.65 MB): the crossroads grid and the side streets fitted, and there is room for more districts. CI run 357 failed on this budget (the first commit of the districts wrote 1.56 MB) and on a catalogue tag count; both fixed in the next commit.

Network: 472 roads, 10 roundabouts, 204 km of streets. Left empty on purpose: the D07 sector (157.5 to 202.5 degrees of the outer band, the traditional suburb: BLOCKED on references in the plan) and everything inside the wall. The route builds in about 0.5 s and places its buildings in about 0.7 s (native). Every city check of the test routes also runs on the city: no building overlaps another or touches a road, corner lots of two roads never meet (a road leaves a 58 m window round every junction, or 20 m where its deepest lot stops short of the side street's own), nobody in the crowd stands on a carriageway or inside a building, every pump is off the road, the mix is varied, a golden digest.
