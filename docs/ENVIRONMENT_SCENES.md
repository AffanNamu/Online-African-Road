# Complete environments for the in-game review (2026-11-03)

**Owner request:** "complete, believable environments rather than isolated assets", and the ten E3 assets (and the rest of the environmental kit) must go through the actual Unity importer and an in-game review. This document describes the review compositions. **They are review scenes, not game content: nothing places them in the game and nothing in the game reads them. The wet versions are not a rainy-season simulation** (no mud physics, no flooding behaviour, no weather: the puddles and the flood sheet are static meshes; RB-18 to RB-25 are not started). The Hausa-style assets have no real reference (M-01), the vernacular forms are not verified (M-14), seasonal vegetation is not established (M-07, M-17): the layouts are design choices.

## The three places, each dry and wet (`tools/blender/env_kit/scenes.py`)
| Place | What is in it | Dry | Wet | Placed items |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hausa-style quarter lane** (72 m) | compacted-earth lane (one rutted-mud stretch when wet), open drains on both sides, walled compounds A and B and 16 m entrance frontages on both sides, a house inside a compound visible over its wall, shade trees whose crowns overhang the lane, grass on the verges | dry shade trees and dry grass | wet-season trees and grass, puddles, one mud stretch | 59 / 61 |
| **Village lane** (72 m) | compacted-earth (dry) or rutted-mud (wet) lane, mud-brick houses A and B and the block house with corrugated roofs on both sides, woven fences along the plot lines, banana plants behind the houses, a rough canopy stall, shade trees, grass | dry | wet: mud lane, puddles, wet-season vegetation | 57 / 61 |
| **Market street** (84 m) | worn cracked asphalt with a pothole stretch, roadside shop rows on both sides, an open market shed, three lock-up kiosks, umbrella and canopy stalls on the pavement, open drains, bougainvillea over a wall, a utility pole with overhead wires (the residential kit's pole), a block house, shade trees | dry | puddles on the asphalt, a sheet of flood water at the low end, wet-season vegetation | 60 / 63 |
Every scene has two cameras: `drive` (the driver's eye, left lane, looking along the lane) and `aerial` (three-quarter, from above the left side), carried with the scene (`env_<place>_<season>`), so Unity builds the same arrangement and cameras from the same data.

## How the layout is checked (`tests/test_env_scenes.py`)
Sizes come from the real builders (`env_kit.layout`), never typed in. A frontage asset stands with its front towards the road on a fixed plane; **no two solid buildings or fences overlap, nothing stands on the carriageway, no tree or banana plant stands inside a building or fence**, ground patches sit with their nominal surface at zero (each patch's datum is measured), every asset exists in a kit, and each dry scene differs from its wet scene in the things that should differ (puddles, wet-season trees and grass).

## In Unity
The review job builds all 26 environmental assets (and the 14 residential ones) from the code, stages them with the scenes, imports everything through the real Unity importer, builds the game with `BuildKanoReviewWebGL`, and drives each scene (`?smoke=kanostreet&scene=env_village_wet&view=drive`). The result, including whether the environmental assets import, how their materials and textures render and what they cost in draw calls, is in `PERFORMANCE_REPORT_V003.md` and `docs/kano/KANO_REVIEW_RUN_41476407715_numbers.json`. **Result (runs 41476407715 and 41489466501): 25 of the 26 environmental assets in the first run and all 26 in the second, all ten of batch E3 among them, were imported by the real Unity importer into prefabs whose LOD triangles and material counts equal the Blender validation (the 26th, the loose-sand road patch, was in no scene in that run; the repeat run 41489466501 had it in the dry village and **all 26 are imported**), and all six scenes were driven in the running game (12 pictures, no page error, no magenta pixel). That is the Unity IMPORT proof. It is not the in-game review: nobody has looked at the pictures yet, and nothing is approved.**
